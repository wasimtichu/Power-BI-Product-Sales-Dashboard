AW Product Sales Dashboard
I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze product sales trends, customer behavior, and key performance metrics. The dashboard provides insights into total sales, order quantities, profitability, and customer demographics to enhance business decision-making.

Key Analyses Conducted:

Sales & Profitability Analysis:
1. Compared total sales ($25M) and profit across different customer segments.
2. Analyzed total order volume (25K) and product quantities sold (84K).

Customer Demographics & Behavior:
1. Examined sales distribution by age groups, education level, home ownership, and marital status.
2. Analyzed male-to-female sales ratios and family size influence on purchasing behavior.

Product & Category Performance:
1. Identified top-selling categories (Accessories, Bikes, Clothing).
2. Ranked top-selling subcategories (Tires, Helmets, Bottles, Jerseys, Gloves).
3. Highlighted products with the highest order volume and their return rates.

Geographical Sales Insights:
1. Mapped total orders by country to identify regions with the highest sales.
2. Provided an interactive view of global sales distribution.

Monthly Performance Tracking:
1. Measured monthly revenue vs. targets (+3.31% above goal).
2. Tracked monthly return quantity and overall sales trends.

Profitability by Product:
1. Identified the most profitable product (Mountain-200 Black, 46).
2. Evaluated return percentages to optimize product quality and minimize losses.
This Power BI dashboard delivers actionable insights into customer behavior, product sales, and financial performance, enabling better strategic decision-making.
